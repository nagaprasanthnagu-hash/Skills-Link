About SkillLink

SkillLink is an on-demand home services and local skilled-worker booking platform, built as a final year B.Sc. Information Technology project.

The platform digitizes the home-services booking process — replacing informal phone calls and word-of-mouth referrals with a structured, trackable, and transparent online system. Customers can browse verified providers by category, raise a booking request, and follow its status from Pending through to Completed. Providers get a steady, organized channel for new work within their category. Administrators retain full operational visibility over the marketplace.

For Customers

Book trusted local help for plumbing, electrical, cleaning, painting, and more — with live status tracking.

For Providers

Receive job requests within your category, manage your queue, and build a rating history.

For Admins

Monitor platform health, manage categories, and oversee users from a dedicated dashboard.
